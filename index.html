Creator Wire: tier and pricing model

Five tiers. Top and Pro set their own rates. Established, Emerging and Starter get assigned rates: the tier gives a price range, the exact price inside it comes from the creator's smart followers, engagement rate and reach. Getting into Top requires all four: smart followers, Sorsa score, current reach (median views of the last ~20 organic posts, fetched fresh) and momentum (Sorsa score change over 30 days), so networked accounts with no real reach and fading accounts can't sit in Top. Pro needs network quality and momentum, not reach: standing decides pricing rights, reach is visible to admins when booking and is priced per campaign.
