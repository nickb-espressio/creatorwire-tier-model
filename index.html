Creator Wire: tier and pricing model

Five tiers, two pricing modes. Top, Pro and Established set their own rates. No suggested prices are shown to creators anywhere. Emerging and Starter get assigned locked rates. Tier gates use smart followers, Sorsa score, current reach (median views of the last ~20 organic posts) and 30 day momentum. Follower count is never an input. Edit any number, everything recalculates live on the full creator base.
